Conceptual design provides a space for doing this. It deals, by definition,   with unreality. Conceptual designs are not conceptual because they haven’t yet been realized or are waiting to be realized but out of choice. They celebrate their unreality and take full advantage of being made from ideas.                                        
Speculative design expressly allows for the counter-cultural, the absurdist, and the humorous.                                           
The prefix “para-” suggests that such design is within the realms of utility but attempts to go beyond conventional definitions of functionalism to include the poetic.                                
Their critical content is hidden beneath  the poetry of construction and the humorous appreciation of their function.
